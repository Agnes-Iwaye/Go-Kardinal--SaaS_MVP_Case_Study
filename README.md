# Go-Kardinal- SaaS MVP Project Plan
 Project Topic: Self-Service B2B Travel Booking MVP

Organization: Gokardinal / Kardinal | Role: Project Manager and Product Manager
MVP timeline: August 2022–May 2023

₦30 million budget | ₦28.5 million modeled spend | 45 → 8 minutes per booking | 60% adoption.

Problem

Manual itinerary requests and consultant responses created delays during which flight prices and availability could change. Customers also needed credit-limit and spending visibility. The baseline was 45 minutes to complete a booking workflow.

Task

Validate the problem and coordinate an MVP for self-service booking, credit visibility, and expense monitoring. The new financial scenario in this assumes a ₦30 million planning budget; this is not the original company budget.

Action

Conducted user research and market analysis.

Developed personas, use cases, wireframes, and prototypes.

Translated customer needs into requirements and a prioritized MVP scope.

Coordinated planning across design, development, and business stakeholders.

Reconstructed the delivery schedule, risks, UAT approach, and adoption measures for the portfolio.

Results

The project advanced the self-service booking solution. The portfolio model illustrates how delivery and adoption could be evaluated:

₦28.5 million modeled spending, or ₦1.5 million / 5% below the assumed ₦30 million budget. This is modeled budget variance, not verified cost savings.

45 → 8 minutes per booking, representing a 37-minute / 82.2% reduction.

60% adoption, defined here as 120 of 200 eligible corporate accounts completing at least one booking within 90 days of launch. 

At 1,000 monthly bookings, the 37-minute improvement equates to approximately 617 hours of booking-cycle time reduced. It does not establish staff-hours or payroll savings because cycle time can include waiting.
